About Legal Case MS

Case Lifecycle

How a case moves
1

Case Filed

Lawyer registers the case with title, type, client and filing date.

2

Status: Open

Case is active and awaiting first hearing or action.

3

Hearing Scheduled

A court date is set. Both lawyer and client can see it on their dashboard.

4

Status: In Progress

Proceedings are underway. The case is being actively worked on.

✓

Status: Closed

The case has been resolved and is archived for reference.
